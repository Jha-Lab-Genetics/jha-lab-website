Nidhi is a master's student in Yale's Computational Biology & Biomedical Informatics program and a graduate of Northeastern University, where she earned a B.S. in Data Science and Health Science. In the Jha Lab, she is developing FiberDiff, a statistical method for finding where chromatin accessibility truly differs between tissues and conditions using single-molecule Fiber-seq data. Previously, Nidhi co-first-authored a paper on an agentic LLM pipeline during a co-op at MasterControl, and she has applied protein language models to peptide-MHC problems in immunology. She hopes to bring these experiences to a career in translational genomics, building computational tools that help clinicians and researchers make better decisions. Outside of the lab, Nidhi enjoys baking, sewing, and going on hikes with her dog.
